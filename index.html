04 · How it works

From your idea to your domain

  1. 01

    You tell me about it

    What you do, who it's for, and what you want people to do on the site. Send your texts, photos and logo if you have them.

  2. 02

    I send you a first version

    A real page you can open on your phone, not a PDF. You see exactly where it's going.

  3. 03

    We adjust it

    You tell me what to change. We fix it together until it feels right.

  4. 04

    It goes live

    I connect it to your domain and put it online. You get the link, and it's yours.

06 · FAQ

Questions people ask

I don't have a domain yet. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I'll tell you where to buy it (around 10 to 20 € a year). It's in your name, so it stays yours.

Who owns the site?

You do. Your domain, your content, your site. If one day you want to work with someone else, you can.

Can I edit it myself?

By default I handle the changes for you, that's what website care is for. If you really want to edit things yourself, tell me at the start and we'll plan for it.

How long does it take?

Usually one to two weeks, depending on the size and on how fast I get your content.

Do you build big online stores?

No. I can set up a small shop for a few products or digital downloads. For a big store with stock and shipping, you'll want a specialist, and I'll tell you honestly.
